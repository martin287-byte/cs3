# Tiny Platformer

A small browser platformer prototype (canvas + vanilla JS ES modules, no build step).

## Games
- `index.html` — platformer prototype
- `farm/index.html` — **Tiny Valley**, a Stardew-style farming game (desktop and mobile) with autotiled terrain, layered trees, detailed houses, shaded characters and a wooden Stardew-like HUD; frost and magma mine biomes, an island reached by boat with buried treasure, eight villagers, and nine festivals; with skill perks, a world map with fast travel, and a first-day tutorial plus contextual tips. Six scrolling areas plus a greenhouse; seasons, 9 crops, sprinklers, animals, a pet and a horse, foraging, fishing, mining and combat, cooking, five skills, a quest board, six villagers with schedules, gifts, dating and marriage, festivals and a community-centre ending. The backpack works like Stardew's: a 12-slot grid (upgradable to 24 and 36 at the shop) whose first row is the hotbar (number keys 1-9, 0, -, =; Tab switches rows; Q jumps to the next seed packet). Tools, seed packets and items all occupy slots; open the inventory (I) to pick up and swap items, read item descriptions, sort (R), trash (T) or eat (U). When the bag is full new items fall on the ground next to you. Your farmhouse is a real interior you walk into (bed, hearth for cooking, storage chest, wardrobe mirror, rugs, windows) and Oliver upgrades it in stages from the shop's House page — Kitchen, Bedroom wing (breakfast from your spouse) and Cellar — each taking 2 days with scaffolding on the exterior, giving +10 max energy, and changing how the house looks inside and out. New games start with a character builder (name, skin, hair style and colour, eyes, shirt, pants, facial hair, random button, on-screen keyboard for the name) and you can change your look any time at the farmhouse wardrobe. Villagers greet you by name (first chat of the day, tone depends on friendship) and the ending congratulates you by name. Then comes a short story intro (skippable with Esc or the Skip button). The touch joystick is analog: it appears under your thumb, speed follows how far you push, and it follows your thumb past its edge. Day and night, weather and seasons have extra visual polish (swaying grass and trees, glinting water, lit windows at night, dust and tool particles). Available in English and Hungarian (magyar): open Settings with the ⚙ button, the `O` key, the title menu or the pause menu (language, music, sound effects, tutorial hints, touch-control size, vibration); a Hungarian browser is detected automatically. Three save slots plus a daily autosave (title screen: New Game picks a slot, Load Game lists them). An axe fells trees for wood (they regrow after about a week; upgradable), and wood is now needed for farm buildings and house upgrades. The shop's Workshop page sells craftable artisan machines (preserves jar, keg, cheese press, mayonnaise machine): place them on your farm, press E next to one to load an ingredient, and collect the product a few days later. Chickens and cows need hay each day (shop, or a silo gives a little each morning): fed, petted animals build hearts and sometimes give extra eggs and milk, hungry ones give nothing. Villagers have birthdays (shown in the Friends tab; gifts are worth three times as much that day) and scripted heart events at 4 and 8 hearts with small rewards. The village has been upgraded with a paved plaza and fountain, a clinic (heal for $60), library, inn, smithy, market stalls and street lamps (older saves get them too), and the world map (N) is now a parchment map drawn from the real terrain, with your position and the villagers' as dots. Add `?debug` for a `window.__farm` test hook and `?touch=1` to force the touch controls on a desktop. It installs as a PWA (manifest + service worker).

## Run
    python3 -m http.server 8000   # then open http://localhost:8000

## Controls
Arrows / A D move · Space / W / Up jump (hold for higher) · R restart level

## Make it yours
- Levels live in `src/levels.js` as ASCII grids (`#` solid, `^` spike, `o` coin, `P` start, `G` goal).
- Physics constants are at the top of `src/main.js`.

## Mobile
Open `farm/index.html` over http(s) on a phone (landscape works best). Touch anywhere on the left side of the screen to get a floating joystick under your thumb; A/E are the action buttons and the ☰ button opens the shortcut grid (inventory, quests, seeds, menu, map, ride, place, music). In portrait the shortcuts are always shown. On a phone held upright the game is rotated 90° by default so it fills the screen (tilt the phone sideways; works with rotation lock on): the ⟳ button cycles upright / rotated right / rotated left, also in Settings. The ⚙ button next to fullscreen opens Settings (control size, vibration, language). Menu lines and the hotbar can be tapped directly, buttons give a small vibration on Android, the screen stays awake while you play, and the game autosaves and pauses its music when you switch away. Use "Add to Home Screen" to install it fullscreen; it also works offline after the first load.

## Hosting
`.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes the `farm/` folder to GitHub Pages on every push. One-time setup: repository **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: GitHub Actions**. The game is then served at `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/`, installable as a home-screen app on phones.
